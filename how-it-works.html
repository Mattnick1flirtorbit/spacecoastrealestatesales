Selling on the Space Coast: The Process, Start to Finish

Every MaxxCity Realty LLC sale moves through the same five stages. Here is what happens at each one — and the Florida timing facts that shape how we plan them.

Stage 1

Pricing: the number is a strategy

We start with comparable recent sales — homes like yours, near yours, that actually sold — adjusted for size, condition, and features, and weighed against the listings a buyer would compare yours with today. You see the whole analysis, not just a number.

The goal is a price that invites competition without leaving money behind. Price is the one decision that cannot be reworked later, so it gets the most work up front.

Stage 2

Preparation: ready before it is seen

Before launch, we agree on the preparation list together: repairs worth doing, presentation and staging, and professional photography. Small fixes that buyers notice get priority; projects that would not pay for themselves get skipped.

Paperwork is gathered at the same time — disclosures, HOA documents where they apply, and survey or permit records you already hold — so nothing slows the sale down once an offer arrives.

Stage 3

Launch: live everywhere buyers look

Your home goes live on the MLS with its photography and listing copy in place, and syndicates to the major home-search sites. The launch day and time are chosen deliberately, because the first days on the market are when buyer attention is at its highest.

From launch onward, every inquiry is answered and every showing is scheduled and tracked. Nothing about the launch is left to chance — or to a lockbox and a hope.

Stage 4

Showings & offers: follow-up is the job

After each showing, we follow up with the buyer's side and report their feedback to you — what landed, what did not, and what it suggests. If the feedback points to a fixable issue, we address it while the listing is fresh.

When offers arrive, we compare them on the whole picture: price, financing strength, contingencies, and timelines, always with your net proceeds in view. Counters are worked deliberately, not reflexively.

Stage 5

Contract to closing: coordinated to the table

Under contract, the work shifts to coordination: inspections, appraisal, title, and lender milestones, each tracked against the contract dates. You always know what is done, what is pending, and what happens next.

As general information, many financed purchases in Florida close in roughly 30 to 45 days from contract, and cash purchases often close sooner; actual timelines vary by transaction. At closing, the sale records, funds are disbursed, and the home changes hands.

Florida selling considerations

Seasonality. Buyer demand along the Space Coast and Treasure Coast is generally stronger in the winter and spring months, when seasonal residents are in Florida and actively looking. Summer and early fall are typically quieter. Your timing should still fit your life first — a well-priced, well-presented home can sell in any month — but the calendar is a real factor in the plan.

Hurricane season. Atlantic hurricane season runs from June 1 through November 30. It is a fact of selling on this coast, not a reason to panic: it simply means a launch inside those months is planned with awareness, and that insurance and property documents are worth having in order before they are needed.

Closing timelines. As noted above, financed purchases commonly take roughly 30 to 45 days from contract to closing, and cash purchases often move faster. These are general ranges offered as information only — every transaction sets its own dates in the contract.
