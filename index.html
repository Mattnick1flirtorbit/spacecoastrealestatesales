Why sellers choose a brokerage that out-works

Selling a home is a sequence of decisions, and each one either makes you money or costs you money. The list price decides who walks through the door. The presentation decides what buyers feel when they do. The follow-up decides whether an interested buyer becomes an offer. The negotiation decides what you actually keep. MaxxCity Realty LLC is built to work every one of those steps deliberately — not to list your home and wait.

Step 1

Pricing

We price from comparable recent sales, current competing listings, and your home's specific features — and we show you the reasoning, so the number is a strategy, not a guess.

Step 2

Presentation

Photography, listing copy, and launch timing are planned before your home goes live, because the first days on the market are when buyer attention is at its highest.

Step 3

Follow-up

Showings are followed up, inquiries are answered, and you hear what buyers actually said — not silence. Showing feedback shapes the next move we make together.

Step 4

Negotiation

Offers are compared on terms as well as price — financing, contingencies, and timelines — and worked with your net proceeds in view, not just the headline number.

That is what hardest working means in practice: no step left to chance, no week left quiet, and a brokerage that can tell you exactly what it has done for your sale and what it plans to do next.

Frequently asked questions

How do you decide what my home is worth?

From comparable recent sales near your home, adjusted for its size, condition, and features, weighed against the listings you would compete with today. We walk you through that analysis before we agree on a list price, so you can see exactly where the number comes from.

How long will my sale take?

It depends on price, condition, and the season. As a general pattern, homes priced in line with comparable sales tend to draw showings sooner than homes priced above them, and buyer activity along this coast is typically stronger in the winter and spring months. We will give you a candid view for your home and your timing — not a promise.

Do you offer a flat-fee option?

Yes. MaxxListing™ starts at a $450 flat fee for an MLS listing and steps up to full broker representation at 1.5%. Many sellers start by comparing the programs side by side — you will find them at maxxlisting.com and on the List With Us page at MaxxCityHomes.com.

What happens after I call?

A conversation about your home and your timeline, a pricing view based on comparable sales, and a plan for preparation and launch. If a full-service listing is not the right fit, we will say so and walk you through the MaxxListing route instead. No long-term contracts are required to start the conversation.

Thinking of selling? Start with a conversation.

Tell us about your home and your timeline. We will bring a pricing view, a plan for presentation and launch, and straight answers about which route — full service or MaxxListing — fits your sale.
